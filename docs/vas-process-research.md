# Value-added service process library

Research date: 20 October 2026.

56 new definitions cover eight sectors. Each definition has five steps and 15 atomic actions. The app now has 126 process definitions. Existing photo-based definitions remain separate.

The public sources establish service families. The detailed activities and actions are our engineering proposals. They are not supplier procedures or site-approved methods. Dates on older source documents remain relevant; a live service page is not proof of local site capability.

Times, frequencies, loads, geometry, costs, and robot performance remain unknown. Specialist operations require specialist capability evidence. Approval steps stay with an authorised person. Chemical treatment is a specialist facility activity, not a standard warehouse operation.

The new definitions can be selected to create a case. The case keeps the source URL, sector, required input list, controls, and output description. The existing review requirement still applies before the business case can be used.
