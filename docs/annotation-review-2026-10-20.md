## Boundaries

Capability combinations are a bounded screening search. They preserve complete handling bundles and retained human rules. They do not establish a global optimum. One-unit fleet counts are placeholders. Known numeric inputs and constraints still control the business case.

Added, removed, and rewritten steps are saved as future-design proposals. The baseline is preserved. The model returns no productivity or financial output for those proposals. A general graph/queue calculation engine is still needed to evaluate structural process redesigns. Actor and fleet variations on the unchanged process use the existing planning engine.

The calculation model is planning-2.2.0. Prior review records require confirmation. The user requested the merge of Matches and Scenarios; seven visible workspaces now replace the prior eight-stage presentation. The separate AWS plan stays additive.

The graph follows the recorded activity order. It does not invent decision branches or recovery loops that the source data does not define. Review the source-path and exception rules before accepting the process.
